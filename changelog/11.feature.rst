The keyword sheet export, ``specs/keywords.csv``, is converted by ``python -m muse_fits_specifications.sheet`` into committed YAML, one file per sheet section under ``specs/keywords/``, which the loader, the validator and the docs read, and ``python -m muse_fits_specifications`` fails while that YAML is stale. The sheet now has section rows, sized integer types such as ``uint16``, ``ISO 8601``, allowed values in ``Lower Limit``, CI- or SG-only level markers, rows in no level and an ``ISP mnemonic`` column; Levels 1 and 2 load per camera type, as in ``load_spec("level1", "SG")``, Levels 2 and 3 are defined, and ``validate`` checks allowed values.
